# S.L.I.D.E. dashboard visual system

## Product character

Practical, field-ready, calm under pressure, and technically credible. The interface is an operational instrument, not a marketing page.

## Visual language

- Forest green anchors navigation and primary actions.
- Yellow is reserved for watch conditions and thresholds.
- Blue represents rainfall and connectivity.
- White operational surfaces sit on a cool, pale gray-green page.
- Use modest 6–10px corners. Define surfaces with borders, not simultaneous borders and broad shadows.
- Use DM Sans for UI copy and IBM Plex Mono for readings, timestamps, coordinates, and device identifiers.

## Hierarchy

Risk state comes first, then current readings, evidence over time, alerts, location, and hardware health. Avoid identical card grids; combine compact readings with larger analytical and event-log regions.

## Interaction

No decorative motion, pulsing dots, glass effects, gradients, oversized icon tiles, or generic promotional copy. Status colors must always be reinforced by text.
