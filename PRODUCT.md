# S.L.I.D.E. dashboard product context

## Users

Community disaster-risk staff, researchers, and field operators monitoring landslide-prone areas, sometimes from phones and under poor connectivity.

## Product purpose

Show current slope and weather conditions, make threshold breaches obvious, confirm alert delivery and system health, and provide enough trend context for a human response decision.

## Safety language

The product indicates risky conditions. It must not claim to predict landslides. Alerts should distinguish observed measurements from interpretation and recommended human action.

## Accessibility

Keep core readings readable on small screens. Never rely on color alone for status. Avoid unnecessary animation, tiny text, and hover-only controls.
